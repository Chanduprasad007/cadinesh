Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers to Common Tax Queries

How does the US-India DTAA prevent double taxation?
Under Article 25 of the US-India Double Tax Avoidance Agreement (DTAA), tax paid in India can be claimed as a Foreign Tax Credit (FTC) on your US Form 1040 using Form 1116 (or vice versa), ensuring you don't pay tax twice on the same income stream.
Who is required to file FBAR (FinCEN Form 114)?
Any US person (Citizen, Green Card Holder, or Resident Alien under Substantial Presence Test) who holds a financial interest in or signature authority over foreign (Indian) financial accounts with an aggregate peak balance exceeding $10,000 at any time during the calendar year must file an FBAR.
Can I complete my ITR or US tax filing completely online/remotely?
Yes! We offer a 100% digital remote filing workflow. You can upload documents securely, schedule video/audio consultations, and receive draft filings electronically before submission to the Income Tax Portal or IRS.
What is Form 15CA and 15CB and when is it required?
Form 15CB is a Chartered Accountant certificate verifying that tax has been properly deducted on foreign remittances, while Form 15CA is a declaration submitted by the remitter. They are required by authorized dealer banks for sending funds outside India (e.g., property sale proceeds, investments).
