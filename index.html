Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers to Common Tax Queries

What documents are needed for filing Income Tax Return (ITR)?
For salaried individuals, Form 16 from your employer, Form 26AS, AIS/TIS, bank interest statements, and investment proofs (80C, 80D) are required. For business/capital gains, profit & loss statements and capital asset transaction summaries are needed.
When is Tax Audit under Section 44AB mandatory?
A Tax Audit under Section 44AB by a practicing Chartered Accountant is required if total business turnover exceeds ₹1 Crore (or ₹10 Crore if cash transactions are less than 5%), or if professional gross receipts exceed ₹50 Lakhs.
How does GST registration and return filing work for small businesses?
Businesses with turnover exceeding ₹40 Lakhs (goods) or ₹20 Lakhs (services) must register for GST. Regular taxpayers file monthly GSTR-1 (sales) and GSTR-3B (summary return), along with annual GSTR-9 reconciliation.
How can I claim DTAA relief to avoid double taxation on foreign income?
Under Section 90 of the Indian Income Tax Act and Article 25 of the DTAA, taxes paid in a foreign country (such as the US) can be claimed as a credit against your Indian tax liability by filing Form 67 along with your ITR.
